Methodology and limitations

  • Package prices and capacities are starting assumptions, not guaranteed live quotes. Verify your selected plan and its current product limits.
  • Product listing capacity means the number of distinct products your store can list, not inventory quantity, variants, SKUs, staff accounts, or orders.
  • Shopify and self-hosted WooCommerce generally support unlimited product listings; Squarespace limits depend on site version. Big Cartel plan capacities differ substantially. Wix package features and limits should be verified for your exact plan.
  • Setup is charged once. Monthly fixed costs and estimated owner time are charged each month.
  • Sales and order volume grow at the selected monthly rate. Average order value remains constant.
  • Payment fees are modeled as a percentage of sales plus a fixed fee per order. An extra platform transaction rate can be entered separately.
  • Taxes, VAT, shipping, inventory, advertising, chargebacks, currency conversion and paid add-ons are not automatically included. Add them to your assumptions where relevant.
  • Owner time represents estimated platform administration and maintenance, not all business labor.
